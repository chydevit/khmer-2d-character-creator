# Mother Sophea (ម្ដាយ សុភា)
Style: A
Status: draft (example character, 2026-09-29)

ROLE: mother / rice farmer (Dara's mother)
AGE: 36–40
PERSONALITY: warm, hard-working, patient, quietly funny, proud of her son but honest with him
BACKGROUND: grows rice at the edge of the village and sells vegetables at the morning market. She taught
Dara to plant seeds and never to give up after a bad harvest.
STORY ROLE: home and heart of the story. She gives encouragement and small lessons, never lectures.

## Visual design
FACE: soft oval face (longer than the kids' round faces), large warm brown eyes (#4a2812) with gentle
  crow's-feet smile lines, small rounded nose, full friendly smile, light rosy cheeks. Skin: warm tan (#c98f5e),
  a little darker than Dara's from working in the fields.
HAIR: black (#1b1411), pulled into a low bun at the back of her neck. Front hair parted in the middle.
  A few loose strands at the temples.
BODY: 1.38 × Dara (about 6 heads tall), sturdy and capable, with a slight working-woman strength in the arms.
CLOTHING: long-sleeve muted indigo blouse (#4f5f86, shadow #3f4d70) with sleeves rolled to her elbows
  and a small round collar; a dark brown ankle-length sampot wrap skirt (#5a3a28) with a simple woven
  stripe near the hem (#8e2f36); plain brown thong sandals (#6b4226).
COLORS: skin #c98f5e · hair #1b1411 · eyes #4a2812 · blouse #4f5f86 · skirt #5a3a28 · hem stripe #8e2f36 ·
  krama red #ab2d2a / cream #efe2c2 · basket #b88a4a · sandals #6b4226
ACCESSORIES: a **red-and-cream checked krama** tied loosely around her head as a headwrap, with the knot
  on her left side (viewer's right in front view) and a short tail hanging behind her ear; a thin
  woven-thread bracelet on her right wrist (viewer's left). No gold jewelry: she is an ordinary villager.
PROPS: a woven bamboo basket (#b88a4a) that she carries on her left hip (viewer's right) with green
  vegetables inside; a sickle only in harvest scenes, always pointed down.
SIGNATURE: red-and-cream checked krama headwrap. Its red matches Dara's scarf, so they read as family.
