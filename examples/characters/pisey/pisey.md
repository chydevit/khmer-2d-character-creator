# Pisey (ពិសី)
Style: B (bright chibi, reference/style-b-bright-sheet.png)
Status: draft (example character, 2026-09-29)

ROLE: child / friend, the kite-flyer of the village
AGE: 7
PERSONALITY: cheerful, chatty, curious, a bit impatient, brave when her friends need her
BACKGROUND: lives by the rice fields and builds her own kites with her grandfather. She knows every
bird's name and loves the windy season.
STORY ROLE: the friend who says "Let's try!". She's great for lessons about wind, weather, colors and counting.

## Visual design
FACE: round chibi face (same construction as Style B), big dark-brown eyes (#3b2414) with two white
  highlights, tiny rounded nose, wide open smile with a small gap where she's lost a front tooth, rosy cheeks.
  Skin: warm golden-tan (#d9a06f).
HAIR: black (#161110), in **two low braids** falling to her shoulders, tied with **red ribbons** (#d7282f).
  A short straight fringe (bangs) across her forehead. No flower, which keeps her distinct from the golden-outfit girl.
BODY: 0.90 × the green-shirt boy (about 3 heads tall, head = 1/3 of height), small and quick.
CLOTHING: a white short-sleeve blouse (#fbf8f2) with a round collar and two small buttons; a **sky-blue
  knee-length sampot skirt** (#3f9fd8) with a white diamond pattern along the hem (#ffffff); pink
  thong sandals (#e27a9a).
COLORS: skin #d9a06f · hair #161110 · eyes #3b2414 · blouse #fbf8f2 · skirt #3f9fd8 · ribbons #d7282f ·
  kite red #d7282f / yellow #f2c230 / blue #3f9fd8 · sandals #e27a9a
ACCESSORIES: a thin red string bracelet on her left wrist (viewer's right). No gold.
PROPS: a small diamond kite (red-yellow-blue with a long tail) held by its string in her right hand
  (viewer's left), and a wooden spool for the string.
SIGNATURE: two braids with red ribbons + the sky-blue skirt + her kite.
