## Rotha — antagonist
- Age 35–45. Clever, manipulative, calm, intimidating. Appealing, never grotesque.
- Face: narrow eyes, arched brows, a sly grin, a pointed goatee and a thin moustache.
- Hair: black, tied up in a spiky swept-back knot.
- Clothes: a dark charcoal/maroon patterned tunic; a high-collared dark-red cape; a dark belt with a
  gold diamond buckle; dark pants; dark sandals/boots. He has limited gold.
- Signature pose: one hand reaching toward the viewer, cape spread.
